# Example notebooks

The notebooks are numbered in reading order. Each one starts with what it
shows, the notebooks it assumes, and its contents, and ends with references.
Run them in a Jupyter session with the SageMath kernel, with the package
installed (see the main README).

## Start here

1. [`01_getting_started`](01_getting_started.ipynb): defining a vertex
   algebra by generators and OPEs, products of elements, conformal vectors,
   weight spaces and characters, printing, and a tour of the named algebras.

## The main families of algebras

2. [`02_lie_superalgebras`](02_lie_superalgebras.ipynb): basic classical Lie
   superalgebras from Cartan matrices or by name, roots, invariant forms.
3. [`03_affine_vertex_algebras`](03_affine_vertex_algebras.ipynb): universal
   affine vertex (super)algebras, the Sugawara vector, gradings, simple
   quotients at integrable level.
4. [`04_ghost_systems`](04_ghost_systems.ipynb): βγ and bc systems, free
   superfermions, their conformal vectors.
5. [`05_lattice_vertex_algebras`](05_lattice_vertex_algebras.ipynb): lattice
   vertex algebras, cocycles, odd and indefinite lattices, tensor products.

## Constructions

6. [`06_subalgebras_ideals_quotients`](06_subalgebras_ideals_quotients.ipynb):
   subalgebras and strong generators, ideals, quotients, minimal models,
   presentations.
7. [`07_commutants_and_cosets`](07_commutants_and_cosets.ipynb): commutants
   and cosets, the GKO and Kazama–Suzuki cosets.

## Further structures

8. [`08_w_algebras`](08_w_algebras.ipynb): W-algebras by quantum Hamiltonian
   reduction, their Zhu algebras, the reduction of modules.
9. [`09_zhu_algebras`](09_zhu_algebras.ipynb): Zhu algebras, their quotients,
   the H-twisted (Ramond) Zhu algebra, higher Zhu algebras.
10. [`10_modules`](10_modules.ipynb): modules, induced and simple modules,
    morphisms of vertex algebras and of modules, twists and spectral flow.
11. [`11_fusion_and_extensions`](11_fusion_and_extensions.ipynb): fusion
    products, projective covers, Loewy layers and extensions.
12. [`12_ope_bootstrap`](12_ope_bootstrap.ipynb): checking and solving the
    Jacobi identities of OPEs.

## Research examples

13. [`13_singular_vectors_in_affine_vertex_algebras`](13_singular_vectors_in_affine_vertex_algebras.ipynb):
    singular vectors and ideals of affine vertex algebras, $D_4$ at level −2.
14. [`14_triplet_algebra`](14_triplet_algebra.ipynb): the $(p_+, p_-)$
    triplet algebras inside lattice vertex algebras.
15. [`15_triplet_modules`](15_triplet_modules.ipynb): the triplet algebra
    $\mathcal W(2)$: its Zhu algebra, simple modules, projective covers,
    fusion and extensions.
16. [`16_virasoro_c0_fusion`](16_virasoro_c0_fusion.ipynb): Virasoro fusion
    at $c = 0$, after Eberle–Flohr and Gaberdiel–Runkel–Wood.
